Exciting updates to our ongoing commitment to support the US Army’s continued modernization of tactical communications through Next Generation Command and Control (NGC2). As the momentum for NGC2 continues, we’re excited to share the news of delivery of DUO LM Ku Sling Blade terminals supporting NGC2 mission requirements, with additional terminals slated to support the 4th Infantry Division. Support and execution from the TAMPA MICROWAVE team has continued to amplify capabilities with the DUO LM Ku Sling Blade from Get SAT. Tampa Microwave recognizes the value of the DUO LM Ku Sling Blade’s modern, low-profile electronically steerable antenna (ESA) design. Fully electronic with no moving parts, the DUO is optimized for mobile SATCOM connectivity on-the-move. Built for extreme operational environments, it provides rapid satellite acquisition, high-dynamic tracking, and a reduced signature in a compact form factor.
