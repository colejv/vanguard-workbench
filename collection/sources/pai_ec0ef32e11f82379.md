      It’s #NationalInventorsDay! Justin Novak is one of the many inventors at SMDC. – Last month, he and a team of 3 engineers from U.S. Army DEVCOM Aviation & Missile Center published patent number 12,535,298 for a weapons system accessory mount. With this mount, the Warfighter will be able to adapt a number of accessories to various weapons systems. By integrating multiple systems a Soldier already carries, they can leverage both capabilities while keeping the exact same combat load, without altering the weapons system or requiring additional training. “When I envisioned this system out in the field helping Warfighters, I envisioned a system that was reliable, affordable, and resilient to combat environments. Most importantly, I envisioned something that could be mounted and dismounted from an existing weapon system to enhance its lethality without modifying it.” Said Novak.
