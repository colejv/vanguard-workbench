Advanced Battle Management Command & Control (ABMC2)
Connecting the Entire Battlefield to Deliver Next Generation Command and Control
Northrop Grumman is leveraging over three decades of battle management command and control expertise to deliver advanced capabilities evolved to meet the ever-changing battlefield. These advanced capabilities deliver the warfighter intelligent capabilities for combat. Powered by robust multifunction workflows that support a wide variety of modern missions, interoperability through modular open system architectures and non-proprietary interfaces, all while maintaining scalability and cloud-compliance leveraging commercial and defense industry standard software tools and infrastructure.
