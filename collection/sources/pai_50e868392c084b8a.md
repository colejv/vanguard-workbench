The Capability Program Executive Command and Control Information Network (CPE C2IN) recognized LTC Herbert Gamble Jr., Product Manager Command Post Modernization (CPMod), during a Relinquishment of Charter ceremony on May 29, 2026, at Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland. Gamble was awarded the Meritorious Service Medal, presented by COL Jeremy Rogers, Project Manager for Command and Control Infrastructure, who hosted the event. Over the past three years, Gamble led the CPMod team through a period of significant transformation, guiding the evolution of command post capabilities into dispersed and distributed nodes with command-and-control (C2) functionality. Under his leadership, the teams’ efforts improved C2 mobility, agility, scalability and survivability across the force, helping shape the Army’s Next Generation Command and Control (NGC2) initiatives by integrating commercial technologies to meet the demands of modern combat. Rogers recognized Gamble’s impact on the organization and the Soldiers who will benefit from the capabilities delivered under his leadership. “You’ve left an indelible mark on PM C2 Infrastructure and the entire command and control enterprise,” Rogers said. “The warfighters who rely on these capabilities may never know your name, but they will rely on the systems you helped deliver.” Gamble’s commitment to Soldiers and mission success remained at the center of his leadership philosophy. “Don’t judge your importance to the mission based on your proximity to the mission,” Gamble said. “Whether you conduct a review or put on the uniform every day, you are contributing to the warfighter. You are important.” CPE C2IN thanks LTC Gamble and his family for their dedication, leadership and service. We wish him continued success as he begins his next chapter.
