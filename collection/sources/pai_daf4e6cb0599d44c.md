Did you know the Common Hardware Systems contract, now in its sixth generation (CHS-6) provides a flexible, low-cost purchasing process to allow the DoD and all military service branch agencies to purchase goods and services from commercial vendors more efficiently? This mandatory commercial Information Technology contract vehicle, per AR 25-1, ensures government entities can easily procure C5ISR commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) and non-developmental (NDI) hardware for tactical and operational requirements. Two of the many benefits realized from procuring through CHS-6 are the cost savings provided through aggregate pricing discounts and the ability to procure emerging technologies from requirements to delivery in just 90 days - or faster - for urgent needs. Stay tuned for a series of informational social media posts on CHS-6 throughout the spring, including enhancements over the previous the CHS contract, and when and where to meet the CHS team at upcoming Army tech-expos!
