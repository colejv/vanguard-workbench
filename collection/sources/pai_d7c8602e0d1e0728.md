# 25th ID's NGC2 Experiments with AI-Driven Command and Control

<!-- source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cpec2in_lightningsurge-tropiclightning-armyngc2-activity-7425295624306126848--h8l -->

“It’s not just a concept; it’s happening right here,” said LTC Andrew Batule, 25th ID Innovation Officer, as he explains how his unit is helping the Army evaluate and shape Next Generation Command and Control (NGC2) to scale across the force through a series of #LightningSurge Soldier-driven exercises. The 25th ID is experimenting with prototype NGC2 data and applications software, which will allow integration and aggregation of data from sensors at the tactical edge, enabling the warfighting system apps to pull the data for the commander’s decision-making capabilities. The primary goal for Lightning Surge 1, held at the end of January at Schofield Barracks, HI, was to establish the commander’s common operating picture, with focus on the secure exchange of data between the map-based Tactical Assault Kit (TAK) and an AI-enabled smart system, and visualizing the electronic warfare spectrum and air space management using the apps software. The exercise also inserted AI tools to rapidly generate data and reports from voice radio calls.
