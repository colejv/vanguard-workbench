# L3Harris wins $24M contract for US Army's NGC2 transport layer

<!-- source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/the-defense-post_l3harris-technologies-has-secured-activity-7382807199422734337-bH2Q -->

🇺🇸💻 L3Harris Technologies has secured a $24-million contract to supply its software-defined data device for the US Army’s Next Generation Command and Control (NGC2) transport layer. The deal covers the delivery of the new AN/PRC-158C NGC2 Gateway Manpack — a specialized version of the Falcon IV AN/PRC-158 — to the US Army’s 4th Infantry Division ahead of their scheduled participation in Project Convergence 2026. “The Army’s NGC2 program is too important to accept anything less than success in execution, including system protection against device compromise, external threats to the network and data spillage,” said Sam Mehta, L3Harris President of Communication Systems.
