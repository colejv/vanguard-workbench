# OpenVPX Modular Open Architecture Solutions Designed to SOSA

EIZO Rugged Solutions designs 3U and 6U OpenVPX SOSA® Aligned video graphics and AI-accelerated computing solutions for next-generation Command and Control (NGC2) platforms. The Condor™ line of embedded video graphics and GPGPU plug-in-cards (PICs) are designed in accordance with The Open Group Sensor Open Systems Architecture™ (SOSA) and integrate seamlessly into CMOSS and VICTORY-compliant architectures—enabling rapid interoperability, scalability, and streamlined technology refresh cycles.
Powered by the latest NVIDIA® GPU architectures, including Blackwell, Ada Lovelace, Ampere, and Turing™, our 3U and 6U VPX modules deliver AI-accelerated parallel processing through CUDA®, Tensor, and Ray Tracing Cores, enabling real-time sensor fusion, image processing, and mission computing at the tactical edge.
Engineered for High-Performance Embedded Computing (HPEC) systems, EIZO’s OpenVPX solutions feature high-bandwidth PCIe Gen4/Gen5 interconnects, advanced networking with up to 100GbE, and flexible video I/O configurations such as 12G-SDI, DisplayPort™, Ethernet, and USB. These rugged, modular designs provide a future-ready compute foundation for the most demanding C5ISR and electronic warfare environments.
