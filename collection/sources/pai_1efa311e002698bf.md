# Anduril Completes Ivy Sting 5 Exercise with 4th Infantry Division

<!-- source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tomwkeane_owntheedge-dontworkatanduril-activity-7439833123854639104-AP0- -->

Anduril just wrapped up another Next-Generation Command and Control (NGC2) exercise with the 4th Infantry Division. Ivy Sting 5 was our biggest exercise yet - and marked the transition from soldiers using NGC2 to owning and operating a hyperscale edge infrastructure themselves. During the exercise, 4th ID deployed dozens of Lattice nodes spanning combat vehicles, command posts, and mobile devices. They deployed Voyager Tactical Edge Compute systems, bringing GPU and HPC computing devices that ran the full NGC2 stack in vehicles, in the field, and disconnected when required. Our goal is simple: build infrastructure Soldiers can run themselves, at scale. We’re proud to support Ivy Division as they continue scaling NGC2. Proud of the team who made this happen. #OwnTheEdge #DontWorkAtAnduril
In any near-peer conflict, you don't get to choose when you are disconnected. The adversary's electronic warfare (EW) units make that choice for you. Treating a DDIL (Denied, Degraded, Intermittent, and Limited) environment as an edge case instead of the baseline operating reality proves Anduril are still thinking like a cloud company, not a combat unit.
