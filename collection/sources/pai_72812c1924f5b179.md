# 4th Infantry Division Leads NGC2 Prototyping with Ivy Sting 4

<!-- source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cpec2in_ngc2-futuresandconceptscommand-armyt2com-activity-7425616767848448000-BI2u -->

The 4th Infantry Division, Fort Carson is executing Ivy Sting 4, part of a series of division-level operational training events that are rapidly advancing the NGC2 prototyping effort. Each exercise has progressed in tactical and technical complexity to obtain user feedback and inform decisions for delivering NGC2 across the force. Building upon Ivy Sting 3, Ivy Sting 4 focused on C2 of maneuver forces from division to battalion, integrating an airspace management application for manned and unmanned aircraft, operating under denied network conditions, and Joint and Multi-National integration with participation by the U.S. Marine Corps and British and Australian allies.
