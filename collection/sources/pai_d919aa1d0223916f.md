# US Army Launches Data Operations Center for Decision Dominance

<!-- source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/us-army_the-us-army-is-proud-to-announce-the-launch-activity-7448415253131939840-ut_z -->

The U.S. Army is proud to announce the launch of the Army Data Operations Center (ADOC), a pivotal step in our transformation into a truly data-centric organization. Achieving initial operating capability on April 3, the ADOC will serve as the operational engine to revolutionize how we leverage data for decision dominance on the modern battlefield. This initiative is designed to connect commanders at all echelons to the Army’s enterprise data, ensuring our warfighters are equipped with the right information at the right time. The ADOC is more than a new center; it's a fundamental change in our operational culture and a key to maintaining our strategic edge.
A data centric Army only has an edge while the data can still be trusted. The real vulnerability is not just outage or theft. It is integrity collapse after compromise. Once commanders start doubting the picture, the routing or the outputs that remain, speed becomes a liability. The real backup is not another dashboard. It is segmented systems, offline validation and the ability to fight from last known good data when the main architecture is no longer trusted.
