# US Army Defense Modernization Through Industry Collaboration

<!-- source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/joe-depietro_balikatan2026-commandandcontrol-futureofdefense-activity-7459972593148743680-ZFzA -->

The modern battlefield favors speed – from innovation and intelligence to integration in action. In my experience, the fastest path to operational advantage comes when industry and the military work shoulder-to-shoulder, transforming mission insight into rapidly deployable capability. Together, we improve the odds of mission success. That collaborative mindset is becoming central to how the US Army approaches defense modernization. In just this past week alone, three efforts that demonstrate acceleration in action: ➡️ Industry‑Wide Integration The U.S. Army’s “Right to Integrate” (R2I) Hackathon sprint brings together leaders from across the industry to host a series of collaborative sprints to advance the integration of critical military technologies. What this means: By exposing APIs and embracing open architecture, we break down data silos, reduce cost, and give our military a single, interoperable network that operates at the speed of digital information. Proving increasing capability doesn’t always require invention, through integration you can make 1 +1 = 3. Read more here 👉 https://lnkd.in/eYT2dQry ➡️ Air Dominance Through Command and Control (C2) During the Balikatan 2026 exercise, Team Lockheed Martin's Next Generation Command and Control (NGC2) prototype fused sensors, fire systems and airspace management in a realistic Indo-Pacific operational setting. What this means: This air-themed NGC2 Lightning Surge, the third of six exercises, marks the halfway point of transforming a concept to proven capability within a year to fulfill specific mission needs. Read more here 👉 https://lnkd.in/eewmYQAh ➡️ 5G Connectivity in a Secure Environment Our new modular, open architecture 5G solution with Nokia Federal Solutions Inc plugs carrier‑grade speed into military vehicles and platforms while meeting Department of War standards. What this means: The plug‑and‑play design cuts integration time, adds new capabilities without disrupting existing systems, and delivers secure, resilient communications where it’s needed most. Read more here 👉 https://lnkd.in/egEBpwSH By listening to the Army’s needs – collaborating with industry to integrate new technology with open architecture command and control operations across domains – Lockheed Martin is playing an important role in the U.S. Army’s modernization efforts that are instrumental in being able to sense, decide and act quickly. #Balikatan2026 #CommandAndControl #FutureofDefense Photo credit: DVIDS.com, Duke Edwards.
